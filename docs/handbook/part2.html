5. Page-to-page connections

A screen is rarely a destination. Almost every screen in this product is reached from another screen, carrying a document reference with it. The chains below are the real ones — taken from the navigation comments and the cross-page navigation calls in the page components. Learn your portal's chain before you write a line of code, because the chain is what the user experiences.

5.1 Procurement

Purchase Requisition/procurement/requisitions→ Approve→ RFQ/procurement/rfq→ Vendor Quotations/procurement/quotations→ Comparison/procurement/comparison→ Approve→ Purchase Order/procurement/orders→ GRN/procurement/grn→ Incoming QC/quality/inspections

Side branches: a rejected GRN line opens Purchase return; an approved GRN posts to Stock In; MRP creates the PR without a human typing it.

6. Step-by-step process flows

Eight flows. These are what you demonstrate at UAT, so they are also what you should be able to click through end to end at the close of every phase.

6.1 Procure to receipt

  1. Requirement appears. MRP produces a buy suggestion, or a storekeeper raises a manual requisition from the Low Stock Monitor.Planner / Storekeeper · /planning/mrp or /inventory/reorder
  2. Purchase requisition is created with item, quantity, required-by date and a reason.Buyer · /procurement/requisitions
  3. PR is approved against the approval matrix. Below the value threshold this is auto-approve, and it still creates a workflow instance.Purchase manager · /workflow/inbox
  4. RFQ is issued to a supplier set drawn from the item's approved suppliers.Buyer · /procurement/rfq
  5. Supplier quotations are captured, one per supplier, with rate, tax, freight and lead time.Buyer · /procurement/quotations
  6. Quotations are compared on landed cost, not on rate. A recommendation is recorded with a reason.Buyer · /procurement/comparison
  7. Purchase order is raised and approved. The number comes from the numbering engine. After approval, changes go through amendment with a revision history, never in-place edit.Buyer, then approver · /procurement/orders
  8. Material arrives; GRN is posted against the PO, with batch or heat number captured for steel.Storekeeper · /procurement/grn
  9. Incoming inspection. Accepted quantity becomes usable stock; rejected quantity goes to a purchase return and a debit note.QC inspector · /quality/inspections
  10. Put-away moves accepted stock from the receiving bin to its storage bin. The stock ledger now shows it as available.Storekeeper · /inventory/putaway

6.2 Plan to production order

  1. Forecast is entered by month and SKU, with seasonality — demand is heavy in summer and the gifting season.Planner · /planning/forecast
  2. Demand is consolidated from the forecast plus firm orders (manual entry in release 1, sales orders in release 2).Planner · /planning/demand
  3. Master schedule is firmed for the planning horizon, bucketed by week.Planner · /planning/mps
  4. MRP runs. It explodes the BOM, nets against on-hand and reservations, treats open POs as scheduled receipts, applies lot-sizing and safety stock from the planning policy, and returns a buy list and a make list. This is a long-running job: it returns 202 Accepted with a job uid.Planner · /planning/mrp
  5. Capacity is checked per work centre against the shift and holiday calendar. Overload is resolved by moving dates or adding a shift, before release.Planner · /planning/capacity
  6. Production orders are created from the make lines, and purchase requisitions from the buy lines.Planner · /planning/orders
  7. Orders are sequenced on the schedule board and material is reserved against them.Planner · /planning/schedule, /planning/reservations
  8. Order is released to the shop floor. Release is gated: no release without a valid BOM, a valid routing and reserved or available material.Planner · /planning/orders

6.3 Order to output

  1. Production order is received on the shop floor and exploded into work orders, one per routing operation.Production supervisor · /production/orders
  2. Work orders queue at their work centre in schedule sequence.Supervisor · /production/queue
  3. Material is issued against the order from the raw material store.Storekeeper · /inventory/issues
  4. Operator confirms the operation: good quantity, scrap quantity, rework quantity, machine, operator and time. Consumption is backflushed from the BOM.Operator · /production/entry
  5. In-process inspection at the operations that require it. A fail routes to rework or scrap, and scrap SS returns to stock as a recoverable item.QC inspector · /quality/inspections
  6. Repeat steps 4 and 5 down the routing: cutting, drawing, forming, necking, threading, welding, vacuum, leak test, polishing, coating, printing, assembly.Operators · /production/entry
  7. Downtime and losses are recorded as they happen, which is what makes OEE real rather than decorative.Operator · /production/downtime, /production/scrap
  8. Final inspection, including the temperature-retention test on a sample plan and a leak test at 100%.QC inspector · /quality/inspections
  9. Finished goods are received into the FG store and the order is closed. The traveller card and genealogy now hold the full chain.Supervisor · /production/wip, /production/genealogy

6.4 Pack to dispatch

  1. Packing order is created for the finished goods against a customer requirement.Packing supervisor · /dispatch/packing-orders
  2. Bottles are packed into inner boxes, then cartons, then pallets. Each level gets a barcode label.Packer · /dispatch/cartons, /dispatch/pallets, /dispatch/labels
  3. Dispatch plan is built for the day, grouping shipments by route and vehicle.Dispatch officer · /dispatch/planning
  4. Pick list is generated and picked from the FG store.Storekeeper · /dispatch/pick-lists
  5. Vehicle is loaded against the loading sheet, carton by carton.Loader · /dispatch/loading
  6. Shipment is created with the delivery challan and, for domestic movement, the e-way bill.Dispatch officer · /dispatch/shipments
  7. Export orders additionally raise export documents and a container-level packing list.Export executive · /dispatch/exports, /dispatch/export-documents
  8. Delivery is tracked and proof of delivery captured. Freight cost closes against the shipment.Dispatch officer · /dispatch/tracking, /dispatch/pod, /dispatch/freight

What the product is, physically

A vacuum-insulated bottle is an assembly, and the data model reflects it: an outer body and an inner body, each drawn from steel coil, joined by bottom welding, then vacuum-drawn and sealed, leak-tested, polished, coated, printed and assembled with a lid. One bottle model × capacity × colour × lid type × branding produces hundreds of SKUs from a few base bodies — which is why the product master is a variant model and not a flat SKU list, and why a body is generic stock until the coating operation commits it to one customer's branding.
